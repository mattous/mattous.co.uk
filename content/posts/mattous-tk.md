I had to move this site to a different domain, freenom who I have to thank for mattous.tk seems to have had some trouble lately. I can't complain really since I had it for free since March 2015. So I've moved the site to mattous.co.uk. 

Theres not much more to say really, I'm disapointed to lose a domain I've had for around 10 years. It would of been nice at least to redirect traffic from the old domain to this new one but from what I've read Feenom is finished. People on Reddit are saying they tried to buy back their free domain names and haven't had any luck, support requests go unanswered so I guess theres nothing I can do about it.

I'll just have to make do with this .co.uk domain that I already had anyways.
