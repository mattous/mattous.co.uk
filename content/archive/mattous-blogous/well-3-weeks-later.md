Well 3 weeks later and I'm back at blogger. Time to make an actual post I think! I just finished reading [Joshua Harris](http://www.joshharris.com/)' book "[I Kissed Dating Goodbye](http://www.joshharris.com/ikdg/ikdgmain.htm)".
