Well I did! As many of you know I don't like having my haircut, which is why it's so long usually! For once I like it though so that's a good thing! I've got an interview for placement next week so I've got to look slightly presentable. I would tell you who I'm going for the interview with but I would have to kill you. Seriously... Anyway the annoying thing about it is that I'm going down to [London](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) on Wednesday for this interview, staying at my house that evening, coming back up to uni for 1 more day of lectures (which I will not be going to because it's the [15 hours of Christmas](http://utsu.org.uk/openevent.asp?eventid=15328)) and more importantly Matt and Mim's wedding on the following Tuesday. Then the very next day I'm going back down to [Kent](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent) again! I will have Pete for company though this time which will make the journey much more interesting!

I'm still amazed by the amount of information on [Wikipedia](http://www.wikipedia.org/) — they have everything! Nothing much to say really, had a manic few days so I'm off to bed. Tomorrow night I'm off out to the [School Disco](http://www.utsu.org.uk/openevent.asp?eventid=13869) that the Students Union is having. Usually everyone dresses up as school girls/boys but this one is special because it's the [Nativity](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nativity) Play theme, so I expect to see lots of shepherds with tea towels over their heads, a few too many inflatable sheep, a few angels and who knows what else! The [CU](http://www.cu-at-teesside.co.uk/) is also doing carol singing outside the Union before the whole thing which will be a good outreach and pretty funny I'm sure because we'll all be dressed up too!
