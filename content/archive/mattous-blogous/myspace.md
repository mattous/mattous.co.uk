Ahhh Blogspot, how much the people at MySpace have to learn. Today I spent a few hours sorting out what my profile and blog looks like over on MySpace — I'm semi happy with it now, happier than I was anyway. Since a lot of my friends use MySpace I've decided to start posting my blog there too just so that I get a little more of a reaction. I'm not happy about it but it's got to be done I guess.

Last night I went to see [Harry Potter and The Goblet of Fire](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0330373/) — overall I would say it's a good film, nothing to get excited about really. It's an improvement on the other films (I know that's not a difficult thing) but it still could have been a lot better. It felt too long for a start, and yet being a big fan of the books I know how much they missed out. The acting... well let's face it they're getting better — [Daniel Radcliffe](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0705356/) does make me laugh sometimes though, it's just that bad. Did they actually have any lessons at Hogwarts this year? I think there was one lesson in the film. They did do a lot of things well though — the special effects were very good as always, the Tri-Wizard Tournament was very well done, and all the stuff that they really should have got right they did, e.g. The Return of "He who must not be named" was excellently done. The fact that it was a 12A certificate this time really did help, making for a much darker film.
