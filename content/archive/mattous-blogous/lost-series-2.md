I just finished watching episode 8 of [Lost](http://www.channel4.com/entertainment/tv/microsites/L/lost/) (series 2). I have to say that up until now series 2 hasn't really been that great. I suppose the first 7 episodes were a big build up to this one episode and now the story can get back into its normal *What the hell is going on*, *Twists every episode* and *Lots of weird stuff has happened but we won't explain any of it* — but I Love it! I've definitely never been this into a TV show before, it's just not normally the sort of thing I get excited about. Apparently the writers have enough material for another 12 years so if you haven't started watching it yet then you've got plenty of time.

Hurley (the big guy) is definitely my favourite character — he's a bit of a legend. And Charlie, who is good in Lost, but I think he will always be a hobbit to me.

I'm off to see [Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire](http://harrypotter.warnerbros.com/) tonight — I'll probably post a review of it tomorrow.
