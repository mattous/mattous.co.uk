Well it's been a few days since my last blog so I think it's about time to post. Had a pretty uneventful few days, nothing really that exciting to say. Yesterday (which is part to blame for the lack of blog) [Civilization 4](http://2kgames.com/civ4/home.htm) arrived. Crazy American spellings. It's a great game, lots of improvements have been made since the last Civilisation game.

For those that don't know, the Civilisation games allow you to run a civilisation of your choosing. Which for me yesterday was the English. You build cities which grow over time, armies to defend against war or go and start your own, research scientific advances which allows you to improve your cities and armies etc. The games take a long time to play — you start in 4000BC and work your way up to modern times and beyond. The game I played yesterday took 6 hours. It's good fun though. One of the new things in this game is they've put religion in the game so I spent a lot of my time building Christian Missionaries and sending them into other civilisations' territories to convert them. This got the Spanish annoyed at me because I had "Fallen under the influence of a heathen Religion" — I think they were Buddhist or something. Anyway they eventually started a war with me which they lost. Their musketeers and cannons weren't much use against my Tanks and Machine gunners, although there were more than a few occasions where my tanks or helicopters were destroyed by guys on horseback... which I think is a little unrealistic.

Another area that's been improved a lot is the graphics — it's all 3D now and there's all kinds of stuff going on all the time that you can see. For example you zoom in on a mine and you can see the little mine carts bringing stuff out of the mountains, you can see the sheep and cows moving around in the fields and you can see your cities grow in size and the improvements and wonders that you build in them. So if you're looking for a good strategy game then I highly recommend this, no complaints really only that it's too dammed addictive.
