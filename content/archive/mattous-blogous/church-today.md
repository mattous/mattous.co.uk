Church today was cool. Got to see lots of crazy Holy Spirit stuff — people with the shakes, falling over etc. I guess it's just something that I don't see that much and when I do see it now it's even cooler! There's a lot of new Christians around too and I like to think how much it must freak them out! God's power is awesome though. I want to get used to seeing stuff like that again because it doesn't happen enough!
