Well I never got round to saying we downed the [Illidari Council](http://www.wowhead.com/?npc=23426) (the day after we killed [Mother Shahraz](http://www.wowhead.com/?npc=22947)) and then Wednesday/Thursday re-cleared up to [Illidan](http://www.wowhead.com/?npc=22917) again. Sunday we spent pretty much all day wiping on him learning the fight — our best attempt was 8%. Monday one of our fire resistance tanks didn't show, but Tuesday we had another geared up and after a few more hours of wipes we killed him! [Black Temple](http://www.wowhead.com/?zone=3959) cleared! Only 6 bosses left in the game that I've never killed! We're not geared for [Sunwell](http://www.wowhead.com/?zone=4075) yet but in a few weeks we'll be going and giving it a go. I'm well pleased with our progress — it took the guild about 2 months to clear MH and BT!

Side note: during our Illidan kill attempt, around 32%, my keyboard batteries died. When [Maiev](http://www.wowhead.com/?npc=21699) showed up I ran to get another to plug in. Thankfully while I was away I didn't wipe the raid. Also had someone banging on the door while all this was going on — didn't answer it, still no idea who it was! Even with all that I still managed to come second on the healing meters. Go me!

*Illidari Council defeated 12/08/08*

*Illidan Stormrage slain 19/08/08*

**Drops:**
- Black Bow of the Betrayer
- Zhar'doom, Greatstaff of the Devourer
- Chestguard of the Forgotten Conqueror (x2)
- Chestguard of the Forgotten Protector
