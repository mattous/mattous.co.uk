Today I finally got an upgrade for my Red Riding Hood's Cloak from [Karazhan](http://www.wowhead.com/?zone=2562). [Gurtogg Bloodboil](http://www.wowhead.com/?npc=22948) finally dropped some healing loot! [Shroud of Forgiveness](http://www.wowhead.com/?item=32337) is way better looking than my old cape — for one thing — but it also gave me 36 more healing, 120 health and about 1.2 more mana regen! I guess those are the kind of upgrades you get when you go from Tier 4 to Tier 6! Best priest cloak until the twins in [Sunwell](http://www.wowhead.com/?zone=4075).

Oh and while writing this post we had 2 tries on [RoS](http://www.wowhead.com/?npc=23418) and downed him on the second try of the day! That's 6/9 BT! Time to go farm for Hearts of Darkness! Then it's Mother Shahraz!
